The Beginning
“What if lip color could feel more personal?”

That question became the beginning of ME-LIPS. The earliest concept explored color-changing lip balm — a product that could care for the lips while developing a pink-red tint that felt different from one person to another.

It was not yet the ME-LIPS you see today. It was the first chapter: an experiment, a direction and a reason to keep building.

Why I Kept Going

I wanted the idea to do more.

The original color-changing balm proved something important to me: personalized color could be playful, useful and beautiful.

But I kept thinking about what the product could become.

Could it move beyond the lips? Could one product simplify a routine? Could the color be buildable instead of fixed? Could two shades create more possibilities?

Those questions slowly changed the product — without changing the heart of the idea.

The Journey

One idea.
Years of refinement.

Every version taught me something about what ME-LIPS should become — and what it should leave behind.

The idea

Color that feels personal

The first concept centered on a color-changing lip balm and the belief that lip color did not have to look identical on everyone.

The learning

Formula, packaging and countless decisions

Developing a beauty product meant revisiting texture, color, packaging, application, positioning and manufacturing again and again.

The evolution

Beyond lip balm

The concept grew into buildable color for lips and cheeks — designed to fit into a simpler, more flexible beauty routine.

From the Founder

Building slowly does not mean standing still.

ME-LIPS has been a long-term, self-funded project built alongside work, life and everything in between.

The journey was not a straight line. There were pauses, reformulations, new manufacturers, new packaging and many decisions that sent the product in a different direction.

But I kept coming back to the same idea: create something simple, personal and useful — then keep improving it until it feels ready to carry the ME-LIPS name.

— Melinda Bui, Founder of ME-LIPS
